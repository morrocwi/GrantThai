# Code of Conduct

GrantThai welcomes contributors of every background, role, and level of
experience — citizens, practitioners, students, lecturers, researchers, and
administrators alike. Treat other contributors with respect; disagree with
ideas, not people; assume good faith.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
language, and doxxing.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, and other contributions
that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban
any contributor for behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.
